Case Study

Mobile Site Survey A/B Testing

A software company tested a mobile-first redesign of its help site. Using a two-sample Welch t-test on 3,365 satisfaction ratings, I found the redesign's lift was not statistically significant (p = 0.149) and recommended against deployment — preventing a rollout the data didn't support.

Case Study

Facebook Engagement Algorithm A/B Test

Evaluated a new video-serving algorithm against the incumbent across 3,611 users. After confirming sample size with a power analysis, a one-tailed two-proportion z-test showed a statistically significant lift in engaged watchers (p = 0.043) — supporting a full rollout.

Case Study

Referral Campaign ROI Analysis

Measured whether an e-commerce referral program across four European markets earned back its €10-per-user cost. Built a counterfactual revenue model over 14,329 users and 24,981 orders, proving referred users purchase 16.1% more often (p < 0.0001) and the campaign returned a positive ROI.

16.5% Campaign ROI (28 days)
